How to use these

SVG = a logo file that never gets blurry, at any size. Perfect for the website and for anywhere modern (WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram profile).

If somewhere asks for a PNG or JPG instead: open the SVG on your phone, screenshot it, and use the screenshot. Or use a free converter — search "svg to png" in your browser, upload, download PNG.
